# Whisper evaluation source

Yamagishi, Junichi; Brown, Georgina; Yang, ChenYu; Clark, Rob; King, Simon.
(2021). CSTR NAM TIMIT Plus, [dataset]. University of Edinburgh, Centre for
Speech Technology Research. https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2994

Dataset: https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/items/30d10e2e-0ccb-483b-b499-8b3c98ad0a2c
License: Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0, as stated in
the depositor's README. https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1.0/

Use the headset microphone channel for app recognition evaluation, not the
body-contact NAM microphone. This corpus contains actual whispered speech,
including clean and cafeteria-noise conditions, from one female speaker.
It cannot establish accuracy for all speakers or parity with Wispr Flow.

The archive is evaluation data only; do not include it in the application.
